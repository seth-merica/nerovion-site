Sound readingUses loudness only, to tell when you're talking and when they're done. Words have their own switch below.
WordsYour phone turns speech into words so nudges follow what's said. iPhone may use Apple's servers to do this. Nerovion saves nothing, and words leave the phone only when the Claude reader is on.
Claude readerSends the last few lines of words, as text only, to Nerovion’s server and Claude for sharper nudges. No audio, nothing stored. Use it on your own calls, never client calls.
Reader passcodeThe passcode set on Nerovion’s server. Kept on this phone only.
